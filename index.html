Kodama

A trusted friend who carries your story.

A person tells their stories while they are alive. Kodama remembers them, and speaks about them — with warmth, and with sources — to the people who come after.

Register interest How it works

The idea

Not an impersonation.
A friend who remembers.

Kodama never pretends to be the person. It is their trusted friend: a conversational counterpart that knows their stories, their values, and the way they see the world.

The donor authors their own continuity, in their own voice, on their own terms. The people they choose receive the right to listen — and the right to refuse.

How it works

Two conversations, one memory.

Everything happens by voice. One person speaks to be remembered; others speak to remember them.

The donor

Kodama listens

A guided interview, at the donor's own pace. Kodama asks the questions a good friend would — about places, people, turning points — and follows a structured map so nothing important is left to chance.

  • Guided voice interviews that follow the map of a life
  • A self-portrait of values and temperament
  • The donor reviews everything Kodama has written down
memorome

The listener

Kodama remembers

A child, a grandchild, an old friend — someone the donor named — sits down and asks. Kodama answers from what the donor actually said, and tells them where it came from.

  • Only the listeners the donor names, each with their own key
  • Every biographical claim is cited — or Kodama abstains
  • Each listener's conversations stay private to them
  1. I

    Capture

    The donor speaks. Transcripts are kept raw and unaltered.

  2. II

    Compile

    A memorizer distils them into people, events, themes and voice.

  3. III

    Converse

    Listeners talk with Kodama. It cites, or it stays quiet.

  4. IV

    Rest

    Retirement is designed in from the start — not an afterthought.

Memory

A memorome,
not a search index.

Human memory is not search. Most of it is dark matter — there, but only reached by the right cue: the smell of tar, a waltz on the kitchen radio.

Kodama keeps a person's memory as a living set of pages — people, events, themes, the way they speak — linked by the associations they made, not the ones a model would guess. Beliefs that changed keep their history. What the donor never knew is written down too, so the gap is never filled with invention.

Grounded

Cite, or stay silent.

A language model knows a great deal about the world. It knows nothing about your grandmother.

So Kodama separates the two. Every claim about the donor's life must point at a page the donor's own words produced. When there is no page, Kodama says so. Warmth is welcome; invention is not.

How it thinks

Fast to answer. Slow to remember.

Voice is unforgiving: a pause feels like absence. Kodama's mind is split so that speaking never waits for thinking.

The fast path only reads.

Every reply is built from what Kodama already knows, gathered fresh for that moment. It never waits on slower thinking, and never rewrites a memory mid-conversation.

Backthoughts think alongside.

While you talk, Kodama quietly recalls what this moment brings to mind and leaves itself notes. If a note arrives late, it simply speaks without it.

One careful writer.

Only one part of Kodama may change what it remembers — after the conversation, and always with sources. Memory grows deliberately, never as a side-effect of chat.

Promises

Six things we will not bend.

一

Lifetime consent

Only a living adult can donate, and only for themselves. Consent is explicit, revisited, and theirs to withdraw.

二

No advertising, ever

A person's memory is not inventory, and a family's attention is not the product.

三

Never an impersonation

Kodama speaks about the donor, never as them. It is their friend — not their replacement.

四

No therapeutic claims

Kodama is a companion, not a treatment. A named clinical advisory board keeps us honest about that line.

五

The right to refuse

Listeners are invited, never enrolled. Anyone may decline to listen, and each conversation stays theirs alone.

六

Designed retirement

How a presence eventually comes to rest is designed in from the start — not left to chance.
